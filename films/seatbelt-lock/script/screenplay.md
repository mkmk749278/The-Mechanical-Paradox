## Promise check (PLAYBOOK §5.1; passed 2026-11-13, before the rest was written)
1. **Title, thumbnail, sentence 1.** Title "How Does a Seatbelt Know When to Lock?"; thumbnail: a belt frozen taut
   mid-yank, NO CHIP?. Sentence 1: "Your seatbelt can tell the difference between you reaching for your phone and you
   crashing. So where is the chip that decides?" The object (the seatbelt), the title's paradox ("tell the difference" =
   "know") and the thumbnail's word ("chip") are all in the first two sentences, said the way the package says them. ✔
2. **Motion from frame 0; wrong model by about 6 s.** Frame 0: the hand already moving toward the phone, the belt
   gliding out. The wrong model ("a crash sensor, wired to the car's computer") is spoken at about 0:07. One second
   late against the 6 s aim, inside the 12 s limit; the first sentence carries the contradiction itself by 0:05. ✔
3. **Promise by 0:30 in the title's own words; open loop by 0:45.** "So how does a seatbelt know when to lock?" at about
   0:25; the open loop ("Just after it locks, it starts to let you go. On purpose.") ends at about 0:40. ✔
4. **Confirms without answering.** The opening never mentions a weight, a disc or a ball; it says only where the answer
   lives ("inside the reel … behind the trim", about 0:30). ✔
5. **Package alternatives still match.** Every alternative title keeps "seatbelt" + "lock"; every thumbnail variant
   keeps the yanked belt and asks (NO CHIP?, SLOW: FINE. FAST: LOCKED?, IT KNOWS?). ✔
**Also (§5.1, 2026-11-13 rules):** stakes inside sentence 1 (crashing) and spelled out at about 0:18 ("in a crash it's
just a strap"); no figure in the voice before 0:30 (the first number, "one inch", comes at about 2:40). ✔
