# Panel

## Top 7 problems, ranked

**1. The ball's punchline comes before the ball is introduced.** It confused four of five viewers.
- Current: "So what locks it? / The car stops. The ball doesn't. / Where does that come from? Below the reel, in one common design, sits a steel ball…"
- Rewrite: "So what locks it? Below the reel, in one common design, sits a steel ball in a small cup shaped like a cone. When the car brakes hard, the cup stops with the car. The ball doesn't. It rolls up the wall of the cup…"

**2. The pretensioner contradicts "NO CHIP?" without saying so.**
- Current: "And there is one more part, the one with electronics."
- Rewrite: "So is there a chip in your seatbelt after all? There is, but it doesn't decide the lock. At the very start of a crash, the same sensors that fire the airbag fire a small charge in the belt."

**3. The catch comes after the recap, so it plays as an appendix.** Arjun and Liam leave here. Move the cost paragraph and "So where do you set the limit?" to sit straight after the Renault paragraph, before the pretensioner. Then the recap "Pull tight. Lock. Then let go, just enough." becomes the payoff of the balance, not the line before a coda.
- Current: "But letting go has a cost."
- Rewrite: "But every bit of belt it lets go, you travel further forward."

**4. It's unclear whose arm catches the teeth.**
- Current: "As the reel pulls ahead, it swings a small arm out from the disc, and the arm catches the teeth."
- Rewrite: "The reel pulls ahead of the disc. That gap pushes a small arm outward, and the arm catches the teeth."

**5. The regulation paragraph is a lecture.**
- Current: "The rules are strict. In the United States, the standard says… The test even has a name. Gravitational locking."
- Rewrite: "And it has to be fast. In the United States, a belt like this must lock before one inch of belt comes out in a hard stop. And it must lock if the car tips past forty-five degrees. No crash needed. Gravity rolls the ball over, and upside down, the belt still holds you in your seat." (This drops the test name.)

**6. "Heavy disc" clashes with "two small weights", and the ending credits the wrong sensor.** In the "try it" test only the disc acts.
- Current: "Two small weights just made that decision."
- Rewrite: "A disc that got left behind just made that decision."

**7. The crumple and mass logic is muddled.**
- Current: "Once the belt stops dead, everything you weigh, moving at the speed of the car, has to stop against a strap across your chest. The front of the car crumples to slow you down. A locked belt is built to hold."
- Rewrite: "Once the belt stops dead, all of you, still moving at the speed of the car, has to stop against one strap across your chest. The front of the car crumples to soften the stop. But the belt doesn't. It's built to hold."

Also fix: "Now the reel locks at each stop" → "Now the belt only winds in, click by click, so a child seat can be strapped in tight."

## Rewritten first 12 seconds

"Your seatbelt can tell the difference between you reaching for your phone and you crashing. Pull it slowly, and it lets you lean. Jerk it, and it stops dead. So where's the chip that decides? The lock doesn't have one."

## The single change that would most raise satisfaction

Restructure the last third (problems 2 and 3). Pay off "NO CHIP?" openly at the pretensioner, put the cost of letting go before the recap, and end on "Pull tight. Lock. Then let go, just enough." That turns the ending into a real ending and stops Arjun and Liam leaving early.

Not ready yet, but close. The story and the twist are strong. Fix problems 1–4 and it's ready.
