**Top problems, and what VO v5 did:**

2. "Imagine you step out" removes the person at risk → "Now imagine the car is empty."
3. The mine study read like a news report → "In United States mines, one study counted at least eighteen elevators
   that hit the top of their shafts. In just five years."
4. The reassurance stalls the hook → "Snaps are rare. Elevators usually hang on several ropes, each many times stronger
   than it needs to be. This brake is for that rare day."
5. The gravity limits → "On average, by at least a fifth of gravity, and by no more than one full gravity."
6. "Take an example elevator" → "Picture an ordinary office elevator."
7. The rail cut buried behind its source → "The wedges actually cut metal off the rail. One maker's manual says…"
Also: "wedges that close on it from both sides" (many designs have one moving wedge); "the European standard"; the gear
trips on any overspeed, not only a snap → "It does not care why you are too fast. A snapped rope, or anything else."
