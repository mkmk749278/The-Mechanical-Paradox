What happens if an elevator cable snaps? The car drops for a split second, then stops within about a meter. No motor and no computer stops it: the fall itself pulls the brake. And that brake is built to slip.

See how an overspeed governor at the top of the shaft locks, how the car's own fall drives steel wedges into the guide rails, why a brake that grabbed as hard as it could would hit you with about 10 g, how disc springs set the grip so the car slides to a stop, and the danger that runs the other way: an empty car pulled up by its counterweight.
