# Film 1 music (owner's choice 2026-11-04: licensed tracks, fetched on the phone)

**Source: the YouTube Audio Library** (YouTube Studio → Audio library). Checked 2026-11-04: every Pixabay candidate
(the three first picks and eight alternatives, 11 of 11) is "Content ID Registered", which the playbook rules out (§11):
a claim on a monetized film. Audio Library tracks are cleared for YouTube by YouTube itself.

**Filter:** Attribution not required · instrumental · 2:30 or longer (or one that loops cleanly).

| Act | Film time | What it must feel like | Audio Library filters to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · the paradox and the cone | 0:00–2:55 | curious, precise; a light, steady pulse under the voice; no big melody | Genre Ambient or Cinematic · Mood Calm or Inspirational |
| 2 · where the cone runs out | 2:55–4:13 | darker; tension building, investigation; low strings or synth pulse | Genre Cinematic · Mood Dark or Dramatic |
| 3 · the price, the fix, the landing | 4:13–6:25 | building, then resolved and warm at the last line | Genre Ambient or Cinematic · Mood Inspirational or Calm |

**On the phone:** open YouTube Studio (in the browser with "Desktop site" if the app hides the Audio library),
Audio library, set the filters, listen, download three tracks (one per act), and upload them to the Google Drive
folder `The-Mechanical-Paradox/train-wheels/music/`. Keep the file names (they carry the track and artist).

**Then the session:** logs each track in `media_manifest.csv`, sets the slots in `sound/score.json` (music ducked
11 dB under the voice; drops 1–1.5 s before "Just shape" and before "neither one really slips"), mixes, and lays
the new sound under the rendered picture without re-rendering.
