1. **Main: NO STEERING.** The hero wheelset in three-quarter view with its treads lit amber: the thing that steers is
   lit. The question it raises: then what turns it?
2. **Challenger: 0.7 mm.** The wheelset head-on, shifted to its flange limit, with one amber pointer at the outer
   wheel's contact. The question: 0.7 mm of what? The film pays it off at 2:23, where 0.7 mm is the radius difference.
   The pointer marks a point, never a span, so it can't read as the track gauge.
3. **Challenger: THE REAL SCREECH.** The wheelset against the outer rail of a tight bend, its treads hot crimson. The
   question: if it's not the flange, what is it? The cause is widely held but still debated (claim C16), so the
   thumbnail says "real", not "not the flange", and the film carries the hedge ("most researchers say").
