# s01 cold open · s03 the promise · s04 the flange myth · s05 the cone (the 3D wheelset scenes)

- **scene_id:** `TW-S01-ColdOpen`, `TW-S03-Promise` (`remotion/Opening.tsx`), `TW-S04-Flange`, `TW-S05-Cone`
  (`remotion/FlangeCone.tsx`). The wheelset is `Wheelset3D` (lathed from `profiles.ts`; research/visual_refs.md).
- **s01** (0–794): the wheelset already rolling at frame 0 (no greeting, no logo), the broken intuition in motion
  inside 12 s; on "both wheels always turn together" two dials turn in lockstep (`LOCKED TOGETHER`) and the HUD shows
  the same rpm for both (ILLUSTRATIVE); on "But on a bend" the camera rises behind and above, the bend draws, the
  inner (amber) and outer (crimson) rail paths draw ahead, `OUTER RAIL: FARTHER TO GO`; on "skid" the camera pushes
  to the outer wheel's contact.
- **s03**: long-lens macro along the rail at the tread; the tread band lights amber on "the shape of the wheel";
  two open loops as oscilloscope strips: the screech (a tonal ring, cut short) and the sway.
- **s04**: between the rails, low; the flanges light amber on "the flanges" (`FLANGE` callout, projected from 3D);
  on "But here is the surprise" the camera drops to rail level and looks along the outer rail with a 4° lens; the
  wheelset drifts the true 5.0 mm of a 1,500 m curve (C13) and the gap left of the ±7 mm play (C09) is measured on
  the outlines at one height (2.0 mm), dimensioned between the projected flank and gauge face, with light through it;
  `NO CONTACT` (C08). HUD `OUTER WHEEL`: PLAY, CURVE R, SHIFT y, FLANGE GAP.
- **s05**: the tread macro, then on "It is not flat" the true-scale section (8 px/mm, generic profile): a straightedge
  parallel to the axle, the amber wedges where the tread leaves it, `← BIGGER TOWARD THE FLANGE`; on "one in twenty"
  the ×3 true-shape detail (20 mm run, 1 mm rise) and `1 : 20`; on "If you move twenty millimeters across" the
  contact moves 20 mm toward the flange and the HUD's radius ticks 500.00 → 501.00 mm (C06).
- **Checks in code:** gap on the example curve ≈ 2.0 mm; 20 mm across a 1:20 tread = 1 mm of radius.
